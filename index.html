Async isn't anti-meeting. It's pro-attention: updates written down and read when people are free, decisions recorded where everyone can find them, and meetings saved for the conversations that need a room.

Write it down first

A status update you read in two minutes beats one you sit through for thirty. Post it, and let people reply when they're free.

Invite only who's needed

Everyone else adds their hourly rate to every minute. Send them the notes instead; they'll thank you.

Make 30 minutes the new hour

Shorter by default, longer by exception. The report shows exactly what ten minutes is worth.

Price it before you book it
